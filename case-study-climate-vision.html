Case study · Climate Vision

Decoding climate risk for homes with Climate Vision.

Delivering an experience to stand out at SXSW and beyond.

Client
Climate Vision, American Family Insurance
My role
Experience + strategy lead
Scope
UX, UI, brand, video, booth, SXSW launch

The story

Making climate risk make sense to a homebuyer.

Context

Born inside Tenney 110.

Climate Vision was born inside American Family Insurance's innovation studio, Tenney 110, with a clear mission: help homebuyers understand how climate could impact their future homes and neighborhoods. Complex information needed to become clear, personal, and actionable.

Role + leadership

Strategy, UX, UI, brand, video, booth.

I provided oversight and direction across experience and strategy, leading UX, UI, brand, video, and the exhibitor presence, and guided a cross-disciplinary team in shaping the product experience from initial concept to conference showcase.

Challenge

Standing out at SXSW.

Standing out at SXSW, one of the world's most competitive innovation showcases, required more than a working MVP. It demanded an experience that could grab attention, build trust, and tell a story instantly in a crowded, fast-moving environment.

Opportunity

340,000 attendees, one first impression.

With over 340,000 attendees expected, Climate Vision had a rare platform to validate the product, build early momentum, and gather critical real-world feedback, if we could execute a compelling first impression.

Solution

One ecosystem: product, booth, film.

We created a cohesive experience ecosystem: a crisp, elegant MVP, a physical booth that invited interaction, and a demo video that distilled the value proposition into an immediate emotional connection, all under intense time constraints.

Strategic approach

Seven moves, one direction.

1

Discovery sessions

Defined core user needs and messaging priorities.

2

Low-fidelity wireframing

Built low-fidelity prototypes to iterate quickly on user flows and content hierarchy.

3

Visual identity development

Crafted a clean, mindful brand system to reflect trust and innovation.

4

Prototyping + refinement

Developed a high-fidelity MVP optimized for conference use.

5

Booth design strategy

Designed a physical space to naturally drive product engagement.

6

Demo video production

Scripted and produced a visual narrative that could tell the Climate Vision story in just over a minute.

7

Live testing at SXSW

Captured live user data and feedback to validate the usability, viability, and desirability of the product.

Impact

What changed.

200+

Live attendees tested the MVP.

84%

Successful task completion rate.

Results

  • Product moved into first-stage funding.
  • Full product build-out and companion marketing site initiated post-SXSW.

Head of the Tenney 110 studio

Supabase · Figma Make · Claude Code

The reprint

A dot matrix print shop-like generator for the AI era. Built to prove that nostalgia + new technology can be friends. Started with a lightweight design system in Figma, shaped the foundations in Claude Code, then brought it together in Figma Make. The real complexity was the print engine itself: generating multiple downloadable formats and saving every print to the wall, a live Supabase integration.

Snifflr

A pet social app built like a dating app. Because your dog deserves a situationship too. Same starting process: design system in Figma, foundations in Claude Code, brought together in Figma Make with plenty of back and forth in the Figma Canvas. Most of the time here went into fine-tuning the design and setting up a working demo account space.

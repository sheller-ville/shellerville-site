Case study · IRONMAN

Going the distance: crafting IRONMAN's next-generation digital experience.

Transforming a digital platform to inspire athletes and fans alike.

Client IRONMAN
My role Strategic oversight, experience design + research
Scope Pro Series launch + ironman.com transformation

The headline number

0K

New users on the Pro Series site on launch day.

The story

A beloved brand, a platform at the end of its life.

Context

01 / 05

Where IRONMAN stood.

IRONMAN, a premier endurance sports brand, needed to transform its digital presence as the existing content management system reached end-of-life. The brand sought to modernize its platform while reinvigorating brand sentiment and better connecting with a passionate community of athletes and fans.

My role + leadership

02 / 05

Where I came in.

I provided strategic oversight across experience design and research, leading our UX team while fostering a strong partnership with IRONMAN's marketing and design teams. I guided the discovery process, established the CX strategy, and ensured alignment between creative vision, brand, and technical implementation.

The challenge

03 / 05

Thousands of pages, one ambitious clock.

Transforming IRONMAN's digital presence meant reimagining how athletes and fans engage with the brand. We needed to migrate thousands of pages to a new architecture while improving the experience, all within an ambitious timeline set by the IRONMAN leadership team.

The opportunity

04 / 05

A once-in-a-brand reset.

With the Pro Series launch and main site overhaul, we had a unique chance to reset the digital experience for IRONMAN's global community: a seamless platform that would strengthen connections with existing participants while attracting new audiences to the sport.

Our solution

05 / 05

Phased for speed, built for quality.

We developed a phased approach balancing speed and quality: first creating an immersive Pro Series experience centered on athlete stories, then transforming ironman.com into a unified platform serving everyone from first-timers to seasoned competitors, with integrated race data and intuitive content management.

Strategic approach

Five moves, in order, on purpose.

1

Discovery

We began with research and discovery to understand needs and pain points for each user type. By mapping the complete user journey across all touchpoints, we gained valuable insights into the emotional connections people have with the IRONMAN brand and identified key opportunities for improvement.

2

Prioritization

We ranked opportunities on an impact/effort matrix and built a roadmap starting with quick wins, then strategizing for larger tasks to ensure we delivered value at each phase of the project.

3

Visual exploration

Working closely with IRONMAN's brand team, we explored visual directions that would maintain brand consistency while enabling more dynamic storytelling capabilities across digital platforms.

4

Wireframing

We created low and high-fidelity wireframes to rapidly iterate on user flows and page structures, validating solutions with stakeholders before moving into visual design.

5

Design system

We developed a design system built to work seamlessly with Builder Studio: custom components that balanced brand expression with content flexibility, giving IRONMAN's team the tools to manage thousands of pages with confidence.

Case study · FS Investments

Cohesive by design: rebuilding FS Investments' digital experience.

Building a scalable digital foundation from scattered product websites.

Client FS Investments
My role Head of Research + Design, Product Owner
Scope Platform consolidation, design system, launch

The story

One brand, scattered across a dozen disconnected sites.

Context

01 / 05

Disconnected sites, diluted brand.

FS Investments was managing multiple disconnected product websites, creating a fragmented user experience and diluted brand presence. The existing headless CMS structure required developer intervention for even small content changes, making updates both time-consuming and costly. This decentralized approach made it difficult for advisors to engage with the brand and complicated content management for internal teams.

My role + leadership

02 / 05

From internal launch to full platform.

As Head of Research + Design and Product Owner, I led a major digital transformation initiative for the FS website after successfully launching an internal sales product. I drove the creation of a unified digital platform that would strengthen the FS brand presence while serving multiple stakeholders, from financial advisors to prospective employees and internal content teams.

The challenge

03 / 05

One platform, many audiences.

The fragmented digital ecosystem was hindering advisor engagement and creating operational inefficiencies. We needed to consolidate multiple sites while ensuring the new platform would serve diverse stakeholder needs, from advisors seeking client-ready information to internal teams managing content with minimal friction.

The opportunity

04 / 05

A cohesive, professional brand.

This transformation presented a chance to establish FS Investments as a cohesive, professional brand in the financial services space while dramatically improving operational efficiency and user experience across all digital touchpoints.

Our solution

05 / 05

Unified platform, five phases.

We developed a unified digital platform with streamlined content management capabilities, supported by a comprehensive design system and authentic visual representation of company culture, all delivered through a structured five-phase implementation approach.

Strategic approach

1

Building partnerships + governance

We established cross-functional relationships, bringing together core teams, stakeholders, and subject matter experts from across the firm. I developed a five-phase implementation roadmap, initiation, discovery, design, refinement, and production, to gain buy-in and created comprehensive project plans that aligned business objectives with user needs.

2

Leading discovery + analysis

I directed thorough audits across content, technology, and user experience while facilitating feedback sessions to identify pain points and opportunities. We managed the platform evaluation process through a structured RFP process, vendor demonstrations, and selection to ensure the right technology foundation.

3

Guiding design + development

Working with my team, we established design principles and a component library to ensure consistency and scalability. I oversaw UX creation and validation through wireframing, prototyping, and testing, while directing a custom photoshoot to capture authentic company culture for the website.

4

Orchestrating implementation + launch

We coordinated phased content migration and quality assurance processes, worked with communications teams on launch messaging, and brought together cross-functional teams for seamless deployment and activation.

Impact

Immediate results

  • Successfully consolidated multiple sites into one unified platform.
  • Received positive feedback from internal stakeholders on improved content management capabilities.
  • Established streamlined operational processes for content updates and site maintenance.

Key deliverables

  • Unified corporate website with integrated product information.
  • Comprehensive design system and component library.
  • Custom photography showcasing company culture and environment.
  • Documentation and style guides for ongoing maintenance.
  • Streamlined content management workflows for internal teams.

Case study · REC

Personalizing the creative gig economy.

Delivering hyper-personalized experiences for creative professionals.

Client
REC
My role
Head of Design + Research, O3
Scope
UX, UI, AI consulting, mobile app

The story

A makerspace that outgrew its own spreadsheets.

01 / 05 · Context

Philadelphia to Miami, on manual processes.

REC, a collaborative makerspace founded in Philadelphia, was expanding their footprint to include a second location in Miami. They needed a new approach to managing their members and spaces as their old manual methods could not grow and scale efficiently. The robust network of creative professionals required a digital transformation that would maintain their personal touch while enabling operational growth.

02 / 05 · My role + leadership

Directing UX and UI end to end.

As the head of design and research at O3, I directed the UX and UI efforts on this project, guiding the team through wireframing and prototyping to UI kit development and handoff. We worked closely with the REC team to envision and build a custom mobile application that would include onboarding for new creators, booking for spaces and equipment, and a directory to connect their creative community.

03 / 05 · The challenge

Scaling the personal touch.

REC needed to scale their personalized member experience while expanding to multiple locations. Their manual processes for member management, space booking, and community connection were becoming bottlenecks that would prevent sustainable growth and dilute the personal relationships that made REC special.

04 / 05 · The opportunity

An assistant inspired by Scarlet.

We saw a unique chance to expand REC's digital platform by creating a hyper-personalized experience inspired by their beloved Membership Director, Scarlet. This presented an opportunity to blend cutting-edge AI technology with REC's human-centered approach to community building.

05 / 05 · Our solution

A phased build: app first, AI second.

We developed a phased approach that included building a custom mobile application with comprehensive member features, then introducing an AI-powered virtual assistant that could provide personalized guidance and support while maintaining the warmth and knowledge of REC's human team.

The work

Strategic approach

Five moves, one direction.

1

Discovery + strategy development

We began by understanding REC's unique culture and member needs, mapping the current member journey to identify pain points and opportunities for enhanced personalization. This helped us develop a strategy that would maintain REC's community feel while enabling scalable operations.

2

AI consulting + conceptualization

Working as strategic consultants, we guided REC through the process of prototyping and validating an OpenAI personal assistant concept. We explored how generative AI could enhance rather than replace the human connections that define REC's community.

3

Rapid prototyping + validation

Our team quickly built a proof of concept experience to test the AI assistant with real staff members, using iterative feedback to refine the conversational interface and ensure it captured Scarlet's knowledge and personality authentically.

4

UX/UI design + development

I directed comprehensive design efforts from wireframing and prototyping through UI kit creation and development handoff. We designed intuitive interfaces for onboarding, space booking, equipment management, and community connection features.

5

Secure implementation

We developed the AI assistant MVP in a highly secure Azure environment, ensuring that member data and interactions would be protected.

Impact

What changed.

<30

Days to conceptualize and launch Scarlet AI.

1,000+

User interactions within the first 30 days of launch.

Experience transformation

The welcoming face of REC now provides users with a conversational experience that ties personalization into the membership experience. All of Scarlet's knowledge is used by Scarlet AI to communicate information about the company and manage bookings with users in a more memorable and engaging way.

Key deliverables

  • Custom mobile application with member onboarding and booking capabilities.
  • AI-powered virtual assistant inspired by REC's Membership Director.
  • Comprehensive UI kit and design system.
  • Secure Azure-based implementation.
  • Community directory and connection features.

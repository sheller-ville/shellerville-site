Case study · Figma

When beloved characters go missing: building the Department of Missing Figpals.

Partnering with the Figma team to create an AI-powered showcase experience.

Client
Figma
My role
AI design strategist
Scope
Concept, design, Make prototyping

The story

Turning Figma's own mascots into a missing-persons case.

01

Context

A showcase for what Make can do.

Figma approached me to create a microsite showcasing the capabilities of Make, their new AI-powered design-to-code tool. The goal was to demonstrate what's possible when designers can rapidly prototype and build functional experiences without traditional development constraints.

02

My role + leadership

Concept, design, and build.

As an AI design strategist, I led the conceptual development, design, and implementation of the microsite. Working collaboratively with the Figma team, I transformed their beloved April Fools' Figpals characters into an engaging narrative experience.

03

The opportunity

Technical capability, told as a story.

This project presented a unique chance to explore AI-enabled design workflows while creating something that would resonate with Figma's design community. The challenge was demonstrating Make's technical capabilities through a concept that felt both innovative and emotionally engaging.

04

The solution

The Department of Missing Figpals.

I developed the Department of Missing Figpals, a playful take on missing persons sites that transformed Figma's beloved cursor characters into "missing" design tools with their own personalities, expertise, and backstories.

Strategic approach

Three moves, one direction.

1

Collaborative ideation

Working with the Figma team, we brainstormed ways to leverage the existing affection for Figpals while showcasing Make's capabilities. The missing persons concept emerged as a way to create narrative depth around characters the community already loved.

2

AI-powered development

Using Make's prompt-to-code functionality, I was able to rapidly iterate on complex interactions and layouts, demonstrating the tool's potential for streamlined design-to-development workflows.

3

Community engagement

The concept tapped into existing community nostalgia while creating new opportunities for user participation through the sightings feature.

Impact

What changed.

Immediate results

  • Successfully demonstrated Make's capabilities for rapid prototyping and functional web development.
  • Created an engaging showcase that resonated with the Figma team.

Personal learnings

This project, and other Make projects I've been building, really highlight for me how AI tools can accelerate concept-to-reality timelines. The bulk of this work was completed in just a day.

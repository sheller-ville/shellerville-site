Case study · FS Investments

Right content, right time: powering sales success with FS Central.

Reimagining content access at scale through research, design, and cross-functional leadership.

Client FS Investments
My role Head of Design, de facto Head of Product
Scope Research, strategy, design, launch

The story

A wiki nobody could search, and a sales team stuck waiting.

Context

01 / 05

Growth outran the intranet.

FS Investments was growing rapidly, expanding its product offerings and client base. While exciting, this growth created challenges for the sales team: they needed to quickly understand new products, tailor conversations, and access the right marketing materials in real time. The intranet, a cluttered and outdated wiki, was failing them. Finding relevant collateral took five to ten minutes, and outdated, unsorted files increased the risk of non-compliant communications.

My role + leadership

02 / 05

Design, plus de facto product.

I served as Head of Design and expanded my impact by taking on de facto Head of Product responsibilities. I led end-to-end research, strategy, design, and launch, advocating for a more user-centered, efficient approach to content access.

The challenge

03 / 05

Hunting for collateral instead of selling.

Sales teams were spending valuable time hunting for collateral instead of selling. The lack of a structured, searchable system hindered productivity, client engagement, and compliance. It was a clear experience and business problem, and an opportunity to lead a solution.

Stepping up to lead

04 / 05

Taking ownership beyond the design role.

After identifying content accessibility issues, I presented my findings directly to the President of Sales and offered to take ownership beyond my design role. Despite it being outside my comfort zone, I embraced the opportunity to drive an end-to-end product strategy that aligned business needs with user outcomes.

Our solution

05 / 05

FS Central, built on Salesforce Lightning.

I drove the development of FS Central, a Salesforce Lightning-based collateral repository. The system enabled seamless access to sales materials organized by product line and methodology, integrated into Salesforce email communications and account records for maximum efficiency.

Strategic approach

1

Stakeholder interviews

Uncovered critical content accessibility gaps across different sales roles.

2

Ethnographic studies

Observed real-time challenges in live sales calls, revealing the urgency of faster content retrieval.

3

UX audit

Identified systemic failures, from broken links to inconsistent naming conventions.

4

Prioritization of features + functionality

Aligned on must-have features: organized categories, optimized functionality, and maintainability.

5

Low- and high-fidelity wireframing

Created a minimalist, brand-aligned framework that showcased marketing materials without competing visual elements.

6

Usability testing workshop

Validated our designs through workshops measuring task completion and speed. Feedback was overwhelmingly positive.

7

Launch strategy

Executed a phased rollout starting with a pilot group, supported by embedded training during sales meetings and clear documentation.

Impact

Results

<5 sec

To find any piece of sales collateral, down from five to ten minutes.

  • Enhanced sales conversations through visual previews, improving material recall and client engagement.
  • Secured broader product leadership opportunities, leading to full ownership of the company's website redesign.

User feedback

"Intuitive navigation, easy to grab anything I need, even in impromptu conversations. Favorites and email make it easy to follow up in just a couple of clicks. Thank you!"

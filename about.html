About Christine: the woman

I've always been drawn to where business strategy meets human need.

That intersection is where the magic happens, and it's where I've built my career.

I'm the VP of AI Strategy + Consulting at Cella, leading the consulting practice and helping enterprise creative, marketing, and UX teams operationalize AI in the work they do. My core thesis: design has always been meant to solve strategic problems. AI just gives that more range.

I built the first design team from scratch three times: Macquarie, SIG, FS Investments. Same job every time, make design a strategic partner, not a service function.

I teach interaction design and AI-native practice at ArtCenter College of Design. Also past President of AIGA Philadelphia, where I launched the chapter's in-house initiative and helped bring its design scholarship fund to life.

Whether I'm leading a strategy engagement or teaching a room of students, my focus stays the same: closing the gap between knowing what to do and actually doing it.

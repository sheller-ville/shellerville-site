Case study · FS Investments

Building unity from complexity: transforming a fragmented product portfolio into a scalable global brand.

From seven scattered brands to one unified identity that powered growth.

Client FS Investments
My role Head of Design, Product Owner
Scope Brand strategy, naming, governance, rollout

The story

Seven product brands, one sea of blue.

Context

01 / 05

Fragmented, and hard to tell apart.

FS Investments faced three core challenges during its rapid growth phase: fragmented brand architecture across multiple product lines, lack of differentiation in a "sea of blue" financial services landscape, and inconsistent brand expression across customer touchpoints. The firm needed a scalable brand platform that could unify its corporate identity while supporting future business expansion.

My role + leadership

02 / 05

In-house plus external brand experts.

As Head of Design and Product Owner, I led an integrated brand transformation initiative, partnering our in-house team with external brand experts. I guided the comprehensive process from strategy development through implementation, ensuring alignment across all stakeholders while managing the complex deployment across multiple touchpoints and business lines.

The challenge

03 / 05

Seven brands, one blue landscape.

The fragmented brand architecture was creating confusion in the marketplace and operational inefficiencies internally. With seven different product brands and inconsistent visual expression, FS Investments was struggling to build recognition and trust while competing in a crowded financial services landscape dominated by similar blue color palettes.

The opportunity

04 / 05

One architecture, built to scale.

This transformation presented a chance to unify the portfolio under one powerful brand architecture that could support rapid growth, streamline operations, and create clear differentiation in the financial services market while establishing scalable frameworks for future expansion.

Our solution

05 / 05

A system, a taxonomy, a governance model.

We developed a unified brand architecture and visual system with scalable design frameworks, comprehensive governance programs, and digital brand resources. The solution included naming taxonomy for products and services, sub-brand relationship models, and measurement frameworks to track brand performance.

Strategic approach

1

Discovery + analysis

We conducted comprehensive employee interviews across departments to understand internal brand perception and challenges, performed in-depth customer interviews to gather insights on brand experience, and mapped the competitive landscape with a focus on differentiating from the prevalent "sea of blue" financial services positioning.

2

Strategy development

In partnership with external vendors, I led the internal team focused on the unification of seven product brands into one cohesive brand naming system supporting two new business lines, and established core brand pillars and positioning with flexible guidelines to accommodate future sub-brands.

3

Creative development

We designed 20+ branded templates to reduce design time for production teams, created a comprehensive visual identity system scaled to support new product launches, and developed a modular design system deployed across all touchpoints including a brand standards microsite and training platform.

4

Implementation + deployment

We achieved 95% brand deployment within 4 months, rolling out the identity across physical and digital touchpoints while reducing time-to-market for new product launches. The deployment covered everything from interior and exterior signage to corporate websites and marketing collateral.

5

Training + activation

I established a cross-functional network of 15 brand ambassadors and led the training of nearly 400 employees with a 90% satisfaction rate, ensuring consistent brand expression across the global product suite.
